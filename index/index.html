SCHOOL CLASSROOM

Our classrooms provide a clean, comfortable, and welcoming environment where students can learn, participate, and focus on their studies.

SCHOOL LIBRARY

Our school library provides a quiet and comfortable space with useful books and resources to support students in their learning, research, and academic activities.

SCHOOL FIELD

Our school field provides a spacious and welcoming area for sports, physical activities, school events, and student recreation.

SCHOOL COMLAB

Our Computer Laboratory offers a comfortable, air-conditioned space with quality computers and comfortable seats, providing students with a convenient environment for learning, research, and programming.

SCHOOL EVENT

Our school events, including Foundation Day, Intramurals, and Freshmen Day, bring students together to celebrate, build friendships, showcase their talents, and create memorable experiences beyond the classroom.

FOUNDATION DAY

Foundation Day is a special celebration that honors the history, achievements, and growth of our school. It brings students, teachers, and staff together to celebrate our school community and create meaningful memories.

INTRAMURALS

Intramurals give students an opportunity to participate in sports, showcase their talents, and build teamwork and friendship. It promotes healthy competition, school spirit, discipline, and cooperation among students.

FREASHMEN DAY

Freshmen Day welcomes new students and gives them an opportunity to get to know their classmates, teachers, and school community. It helps freshmen feel comfortable, make new friends, and begin their college journey with confidence.
